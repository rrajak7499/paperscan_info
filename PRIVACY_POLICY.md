# Privacy Policy for PaperScan: Smart PDF Scanner

**Effective Date:** November 1, 2026  
**Last Updated:** November 1, 2026

PaperScan ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our data practices for the **PaperScan: Smart PDF Scanner** mobile application.

---

### 1. On-Device Document Processing
PaperScan processes all your scanned paper documents, Optical Character Recognition (OCR), document edge detection, page ordering, and PDF generation **entirely on your local device**. Your scanned documents and images never leave your phone.

---

### 2. Advertising & Third-Party Services
PaperScan uses **Google AdMob** to display advertisements. 
- **Google AdMob:** May collect device identifiers (such as Advertising ID), IP addresses, and coarse location for the purpose of serving personalized or non-personalized advertisements and preventing fraud.
- You can learn more about how Google handles user data by reviewing [Google's Privacy & Terms](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

---

### 3. Device Permissions Used
- **Camera (`android.permission.CAMERA`):** Required solely to capture photos of paper documents for scanning.
- **Storage / Media Access:** Used to import images from your photo gallery or save exported PDF files.
- **Internet (`android.permission.INTERNET`):** Used strictly by Google AdMob to load advertisements.

---

### 4. Children's Privacy
PaperScan does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
