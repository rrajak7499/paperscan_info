Privacy Policy for PaperScan: Smart PDF Scanner

Effective Date: November 1, 2026

Document Privacy: All document scanning, OCR, and PDF exports are processed 100% locally on your device.

1. Document Security

Your scanned documents, images, and text extracts stay strictly on your device and are never uploaded to any server.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

PaperScan displays ads using Google AdMob. Google may use device identifiers and network access to deliver advertisements in compliance with Google's Advertising Policies.

3. Permissions Used

  • Camera: Used to capture physical documents.
  • Storage: Used to load/export files.
  • Internet: Used strictly to fetch advertisements via Google AdMob.

4. Contact Us

For support or privacy inquiries, email us at support@paperscan.com.
